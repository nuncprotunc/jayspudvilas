## Pareto Optimal Characteristics (Highest Success Factors)

### What ACTUALLY Matters (Based on Acceptances):

1. **Message Quality > Predicted Success Rate**
   - 60% predicted rate converted just as well as 100% predicted rate
   - Focus on authenticity and specificity, not gaming the probability

2. **Specificity > Mutual Connections**
   - 0 mutual connections converted successfully when message was specific
   - 21 mutual connections converted with lower predicted rate
   - **Key**: Name specific work, not just their title

3. **Authenticity Calibration > Relevance**
   - LOW relevance (family law) converted
   - LOW-MODERATE relevance (tertiary education) converted

   - "I'm curious how..." worked across ALL contacts
   - **Key**: Position as learner, not networker
